1Enrolment

Collect it from the department office (Room 204) between 10 am and 4 pm. Bring your admission receipt and one passport photo. Cards are ready within three working days. A lost card can be replaced for Rs. 100 with a written application.

Hand the elective change form to your class coordinator within the first two weeks of the semester. Changes are approved only if seats remain in the new elective. After the window closes, you can switch next semester.

Fill in the request form at the office and attach a copy of your ID card. It is issued in two working days. Write the purpose (bank, passport or scholarship) on the form to avoid delays.

2Exams

Hall tickets appear on the student portal about seven days before the exams start. You need cleared fees and at least 75 percent attendance. Print two copies and carry one with your ID card.

Apply on the portal within five days of the result, one application per subject, and pay the revaluation fee. Keep the receipt number. Revised marks are published in about two weeks, and the higher score is kept.

Give the exam cell a medical certificate and a supplementary exam application within three days of the missed paper. Approved students sit the supplementary exam within the following month.

3Fees

Fees are due by the 15th of the first month of each semester. After that a late fee of Rs. 50 a day applies, up to Rs. 1,000. You can pay online or at the accounts window.

Check the list of schemes on the notice board or portal. Submit the application with your income certificate, last marksheet and a copy of your bank passbook to the accounts office before the notified deadline.

It depends on timing: full refund before classes begin, partial refund in the first month, and none after that. Submit a written withdrawal application with your fee receipt to the accounts office.

4Labs and library

Labs run Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 5 pm, in the batches shown on your timetable. Open lab hours with a lab assistant are 4 to 6 pm on weekdays, for practice and unfinished experiments.

Three books for 14 days each. You can renew once online if nobody has reserved the title. Late returns cost Rs. 2 a day per book.

Ask the IT desk for credentials and show your ID card. Accounts are active within a day. Your username is your enrolment number, and you must change the default password the first time you log in.
